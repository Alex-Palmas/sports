    Arbitrage & middles

    Pairs of an over at one book and an under at another priced so that you profit whatever happens. A middle has a lower over line than under line, so both legs win if the result lands in between.

    Kalshi Latency Study

    Does a free live feed see NBA plays before Kalshi reprices? For every play that settles a Kalshi player prop (say, a basket that takes a player past 20 points), we time when it happened, when the feed delivered it, and when Kalshi's price caught up. Read-only research: nothing here places orders.

    How it's measured
    • Play time is the wall-clock stamp the feed attaches to each play (ESPN's wallclock, the NBA feed's timeActual).
    • Feed delay is when our poller (about once a second, from a GitHub server) first saw the play, minus the play time.
    • Kalshi adjusts when the YES bid on the settled market first reaches 90¢ or Kalshi closes the market. Kalshi's own update stamp is used when it falls between two polls; otherwise the poll time.
    • Stale price is the YES ask Kalshi still showed when the feed delivered the play, and the profit per contract if bought then and settled at $1, after Kalshi's fee. It ignores how long your order takes to arrive and how many contracts were offered.
    • Game line moves is how long Kalshi's game-winner market took to change price after a scoring play.
    • Polling once a second adds up to about a second of error to each timing. Plays that were already priced at 90¢ or more beforehand are excluded.
